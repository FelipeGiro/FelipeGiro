# Felipe Giro

 🚢 **Naval Architect** | ⚙️ **Engineer** | 💻 **Data Scientist** | 🎓 **PhD in Offshore Engineering and Probabilistic Modeling**

I am a naval architect with a strong background in data science, scientific computing, and probabilistic modeling. My experience spans engineering consultancy, software development, and academic research.

During my PhD, I specialized in probabilistic modeling and structural reliability for offshore wind structures, with particular emphasis on Bayesian inference and uncertainty quantification.

Today, I focus on combining engineering expertise with machine learning and modern software development to turn complex data into reliable, practical solutions.

For more details about my professional experience and academic background, please visit my [LinkedIn profile](https://www.linkedin.com/in/felipegiro/).

## Featured Projects

### 📊 [Rio Real Estate Explorer](https://github.com/FelipeGiro/rio-real-state-explorer) (in development)
An applied data science project exploring Rio de Janeiro's real estate market through data analysis, visualization, and machine learning. Demonstrates predictive modeling and the interpretation of real-world housing data.

### 🌊 [sysBUS — Offshore Structural Reliability](https://github.com/FelipeGiro/sysBUS)
Research software associated with my PhD, focused on probabilistic modeling and structural reliability. Demonstrates the application of Bayesian inference and uncertainty quantification to complex engineering problems.
The detailed description of the method can be found [here](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0951833925002205)

### 📡 [CSNUKF — Closed Skew-Normal Unscented Kalman Filter](https://github.com/FelipeGiro/csnukf)
An implementation of a nonlinear state estimation method using the closed skew-normal distribution, as in [Rezaie, J., & Eidsvik, J. (2016). A skewed unscented Kalman filter](https://doi.org/10.1080/00207179.2016.1171912). 
Explores filtering beyond conventional Gaussian assumptions, with potential applications in tracking, navigation, and sensor fusion.
